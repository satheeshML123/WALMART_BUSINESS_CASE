# Black Friday Purchase Behavior Analysis – Walmart Inc.
 Walmart : Confidence Interval & CLT

 Project Overview:
This project focuses on analyzing customer purchase behavior at Walmart Inc., with a special emphasis on the purchase amount in relation to customer gender and other demographic factors. The goal is to extract actionable insights that support data-driven business decisions.

Objective:
To determine whether there is a significant difference in spending habits between male and female customers during Black Friday sales. The analysis assumes a customer base of 50 million males and 50 million females.

Key Analysis Areas:

Compare average purchase amounts by gender

Identify high-spending customer segments

Explore other influencing factors (e.g., age, city category, marital status)

Provide visual insights using tools like Python (Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib) or Power BI/Tableau

Outcome:
Deliver data-driven recommendations to the Walmart management team on customer segmentation, marketing strategies, and sales forecasting based on gendered purchase patterns during high-demand sales events like Black Friday.
